I'm an IT Architect II at WCB-Alberta, in Edmonton. I've been there since 2019, first as a specialist on the Infrastructure Platform team and in the architecture group since 2022.

The work is mostly Microsoft. I designed WCB-Alberta's first Azure tenant and landing zone, and before that spent several years running the platforms underneath it: Exchange, SharePoint, Citrix, VMware. Lately more of it is Power Platform and Dynamics 365, along with the governance that goes with a growing low-code estate.

I hold the Azure Solutions Architect Expert certification and four others. I work in English and Chinese.

Most of what I write down is internal: role definitions, naming conventions, the reasoning behind a network topology. The parts that generalise end up on the writing page.
