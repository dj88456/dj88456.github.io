About

More than the job title, but the job title is a good start

I'm an IT Architect II at WCB-Alberta, in Edmonton. Nine years in, most of my work sits at the point where a decision stops being technical and starts being organisational: who gets access to what, which region the data lives in, what happens when the team that built a thing moves on.

My background is Microsoft-shaped. I led WCB-Alberta's first Azure tenant and landing zone design, and I've spent years on the enterprise platforms underneath it — Exchange, SharePoint, Citrix, VMware — long enough to have opinions about all of them. More recently the centre of gravity has moved toward Power Platform and Dynamics 365, and toward the governance work that keeps a fast-growing low-code estate from quietly becoming somebody else's problem.

I hold the Azure Solutions Architect Expert certification along with four other Microsoft and industry certifications. I work in English and Chinese, which turns out to be useful more often than you would expect.

The thing I care about most is legibility. A system that only one person understands is a system with a single point of failure, and that person is usually the one who built it. So I write things down: role definitions, naming conventions, the reasoning behind a topology. Some of that ends up on the writing page.
