Four projects worth writing down

Most of what I do leaves no visible trace. These are the pieces that changed how a whole organisation works, so they are the ones I can actually describe.

01 Azure Landing Zone WCB-Alberta had no Azure presence at all. I designed the tenant from the ground up: management group hierarchy, platform and workload subscriptions, a hub-and-spoke network with Azure Firewall, a tiered RBAC model, and the policy set that keeps everything inside Canadian regions. Azure · Landing Zone · RBAC · Azure Policy WCB-Alberta
2020–2022 02 Exchange Online Migration Moving 1,500 mailboxes off on-premises Exchange without anyone noticing. Hybrid configuration first, then eight weeks of wave migrations, with compliance holds and mail flow held intact throughout. Exchange Online · Microsoft 365 · Hybrid WCB-Alberta
Completed 03 Dynamics 365 & Power Platform Environment strategy across dev, test and production, Azure AD integration, Power BI for enterprise reporting, and a Centre of Excellence so a low-code estate could grow without becoming shadow IT. Dynamics 365 · Power BI · Governance WCB-Alberta
